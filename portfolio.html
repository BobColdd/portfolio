About

I graduated with a BSc in Information Technology from the Technical University of Mombasa in 2025, with my strongest academic performance in databases, data structures, and systems design — the areas I've since turned into my professional focus.

Since graduating, I've been building in parallel: deploying real infrastructure for a community education centre in Nairobi, building data pipelines for sports analytics, and publishing technical writing on data engineering through my publication Cold Informatica. I don't separate learning from building — every project is a production deployment.

My long-term direction is data engineering and data infrastructure — specifically the pipeline layer: ingestion, transformation, storage, and the systems that make data queryable at scale. I'm drawn to problems where the data itself is the constraint, not just the downstream model or dashboard.

Outside engineering, I run Coldd Analytics, a football analytics brand producing stat cards and match intelligence content on Instagram. It keeps my data instincts sharp and my design eye honest.
